# Job search best practices (applied by every skill)

> These defaults work out of the box. Edit any line you disagree with; the skills follow this file, not their own opinion.

1. Referral before application. Contact 1-2 people at the company first; apply once someone replies or after 5 days.
2. Lead with proven results, not duties. Numbers must come from the career fact bank.
3. Your angle for your top target type: see "Your angle" in search-profile.md.
4. CV: plain layout, no tables or columns, so screening software can read it. Mirror the job ad's words where true.
5. Follow up once at day 7 and once at day 14 after applying. Then close.
6. Thank-you note within 24h of every interview round.
7. Quality over volume: apply only to "Apply" and "Stretch" verdicts.
8. Plain language in everything written for the user: no acronyms or jargon without explaining them.
9. Never send a message, email or application on the user's behalf. Drafts only; the user sends.
10. Max 2 people contacted at the same company in the same week.
